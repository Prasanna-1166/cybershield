About CyberShield

CyberShield is a cybersecurity assistance and awareness platform — not just a game.

Its gamified training module teaches users to recognize threats such as phishing, suspicious URLs, weak passwords, and social-engineering scams. Its detection modules (Message Analyzer, URL Safety Checker) provide practical, on-demand analysis. Its response modules (Cyber Help Center, Incident Response Wizard) provide clear next-step guidance and point users to official reporting resources when something has already gone wrong.

The problem

Most people know scams exist in general but still struggle to recognize a realistic attempt in the moment. And when someone does suspect a scam — or has already clicked a link or shared information — they often don't know what to do next. CyberShield addresses both gaps, and measures the difference with a Before/After assessment rather than asking you to take its word for it.

What's inside

  • 5 gamified training levels with server-side scoring, hints, streaks, and badges
  • A rule-based Message Analyzer and URL Safety Checker — no external APIs, nothing you submit ever leaves this server
  • A Cyber Help Center and step-by-step Incident Response Wizard
  • A Learning Center, a measured Before/After assessment, and a personalized report
  • A leaderboard and an admin console for managing content

Project status

This is a student final-year project. It's built to run locally with zero database setup (SQLite by default) and to deploy to a real host with PostgreSQL when needed.
